# Code of conduct

Participants should communicate respectfully, welcome constructive feedback,
and focus disagreements on technical ideas rather than people.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, disclosure of private information, and
repeated disruptive behavior are not acceptable in issues, pull requests,
discussions, or other project spaces.

Maintainers may moderate content, close disruptive threads, or restrict participation
according to the severity and repetition of behavior. Apply these standards fairly
and protect the privacy of people reporting concerns.

For private concerns, ask maintainers for a private reporting channel without
posting identifying details publicly. Security vulnerability reports follow
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
